# Project 2 — Lead-to-Opportunity Revenue Operations System

## Project Type

Independent professional Revenue Systems implementation / synthetic case study.

## Synthetic Company

RelayIQ is a B2B SaaS company using a hybrid sales-led revenue motion.

Leads enter the revenue system through multiple acquisition channels, including:

- Demo requests
- Website forms
- Partner referrals
- Outbound prospecting

## Business Problem

Lead intake and sales follow-up are operationally inconsistent.

Key problems include:

- incomplete or inconsistent lead data
- duplicate records
- unclear qualification rules
- inconsistent sales routing
- delayed sales follow-up
- poor SLA visibility
- duplicate Account or Opportunity creation
- limited operational traceability

## System Goal

Design and implement an end-to-end Lead-to-Opportunity Revenue Operations system that:

1. receives and validates incoming leads
2. normalizes critical lead data
3. evaluates fit and qualification
4. routes qualified leads to the correct sales motion
5. tracks sales response SLA
6. controls escalation when SLA is breached
7. manages Salesforce Lead-to-Account/Contact/Opportunity progression
8. prevents duplicate commercial records
9. records processing and failure states
10. provides auditable operational evidence

## Primary CRM

Salesforce

## Supporting Stack

- Salesforce
- n8n
- PostgreSQL / Supabase
- REST APIs
- JavaScript

## Portfolio Positioning

This project demonstrates Salesforce-centered Revenue Systems engineering across lead management, qualification, routing, SLA management, lifecycle control, reliability, and operational governance.
